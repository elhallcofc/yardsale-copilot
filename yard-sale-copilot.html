Import a map

Step 1 — from a Google My Maps link (skip if you already have the file). Paste the link and tap Download; your browser saves the map as a .kml file.

Step 2 — pick the file. Works with .kml from Google My Maps / Google Earth, or a .txt/.csv list with one address per line.

How it works

  1. Add stops one at a time or paste a whole list.
  2. Tap ✨ Plan route to order them into the shortest trip from where you are.
  3. Hit Go to drive there with your maps app. The Today tab shows the whole day: every stop in order, drive times, and when you'll finish.
  4. Tap Arrived at each sale. 🚩 anything worth a second look.

Everything is saved on this device only. Use "Copy list to share" to send your list to someone else — they can paste it right in.
